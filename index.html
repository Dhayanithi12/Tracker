September — this month's UPSC materials

Not the general resource dump below — specifically what Weeks 1–4 of the plan actually put in front of you this month, so you're never starting a block without knowing what to open. This card is dated; it'll need re-mapping once October's rotation changes the topics.

Getting oriented — Day 1 & Week 1
Why Psychology, and how to prep it — Dev Shree (YouTube). Three short orientation videos worth watching back-to-back before you write your first answer: "Why to Choose Psychology Optional", "Preparation Strategy for Psychology Optional", and "How to write effective answers for Psychology Optional" — that last one is the one to rewatch before your first timed answer on 6 Sep.
Psychology optional — syllabus overview. Drishti IAS's own breakdown of the full Paper 1 + Paper 2 syllabus, good as a second reference alongside the official UPSC syllabus PDF when you're mapping it against your MSc on Day 1. drishtiias.com/upsc-optional-subjects/psychology
Optional Paper 1 — Foundations (cycling all month)
Psyche Simplified — channel. Your main video source for Paper 1 units as they rotate through September (Introduction, Methods, Research Methods & Stats, and onward). youtube.com/@psychesimplifiedofficial
Paper 1, Unit 1 — micro-syllabus walkthrough. A concrete starting video for exactly where Paper 1 begins. "1.1 Psychology optional (UPSC) — Paper 1 Unit 1 Micro-syllabus"
PYQs, topic-wise, 7 years — Psycho IAS (playlist, 14 videos). Watch the relevant video right after you read a new unit, not before — it shows you exactly how UPSC has actually asked about that topic, which should shape how you write your timed answers. youtube.com — "UPSC PYQs Psychology Optional, Topic-wise"
Optional Paper 2 — Applied (Work / Educational / Community Psychology this month)
Be realistic about this one: free, dedicated video content for Paper 2 is genuinely thin compared to Paper 1 — most of the well-known channels lean Paper-1-heavy. Budget more of your own reading time here rather than expecting a ready-made lecture for every unit.
EduRev — Psychology for UPSC Optional (notes + flashcards). Best free-tier match for Paper 2's specific units (Work, Educational, Community Psychology this month) — use it as your primary written source and treat video as supplementary. edurev.in
Answer-writing format still applies from Paper 1: Dev Shree's "How to write effective answers" video above is written generally — it applies just as much to your Paper 2 answers this month.
CSAT — diagnostic + first drills (Week 1 onward)
Sleepy Classes — free CSAT course. Seven full YouTube playlists (core CSAT, weekly aptitude, number system from basics, reasoning, aptitude tricks, practice series) — genuinely free, no signup wall found. This is your main CSAT source for the month. sleepyclasses.com/free-upsc-csat-course
CSAT Maths — free complete playlist. If Sleepy Classes' number-system playlist doesn't click, this is a solid alternate. youtube.com — "CSAT Maths Free Complete Online Classes"
GS I overlap via EPFO this month (Art & Culture, Freedom Movement)
Use the official NCERT link above, these three specifically: Class 11 An Introduction to Indian Art (for the Art & Architecture week), Class 8 Our Pasts—III and Class 12 Themes in Indian History, Part III (for the Freedom Movement weeks). These are the same static-GS content EPFO is already making you read — don't buy a coaching book for this until you've actually read these free ones first.
On Nitin Singhania / Spectrum / Bipan Chandra: these are the standard commercial references for Art & Culture and Modern History (already named under Reference books below) — worth owning eventually, but the free NCERT chapters above cover this month's actual EPFO topics well enough not to need them yet.

Learning materials

Official sources are linked directly and are genuinely free with no login. A few others need a free account to unlock a PDF — flagged where that's true. Nothing here is a guessed link to a specific file; these are the hub pages so you always land on the current year's version.

Official — free, no login
UPSC — official. Syllabus, notifications, calendar, results, and every past Prelims + Mains question paper as direct PDF. upsc.gov.in · PYQs specifically: upsc.gov.in/examinations/previous-question-papers
NCERT — official. Every NCERT textbook (Class 6–12, all subjects) as a free PDF, no signup. This is the actual foundation layer under EPFO's static GS and CSE GS — read these before any coaching-institute notes. ncert.nic.in/textbook.php
IBPS — official. RRB PO notification, admit cards, results. ibps.in
EPFO — official. APFC notification and updates. epfo.gov.in
PIB — official. Government press releases, the base layer for current affairs. pib.gov.in
PRS Legislative Research. Plain-language bill and policy summaries — useful for both EPFO's Labour Codes and CSE's GS II/III. prsindia.org
Sansad TV. Official Parliament channel on YouTube — live/recorded proceedings and discussions, genuinely useful for EPFO Governance and CSE GS II, not just background noise.
Previous-year papers (PDF) — for CSE, RRB, EPFO, IB ACIO, CSAT
UPSC CSE PYQs — official, all years. Use the upsc.gov.in link above; it's the single most reliable source, no third party needed.
CSAT PYQs, 2014–current, compiled. pwonlyias.com/csat-previous-year-question-papers
EPFO APFC PYQs, compiled with solutions. pwonlyias.com/epfo-previous-year-question-paper
RRB PO PYQs, compiled with solutions. ibpsguide.com/ibps-rrb-previous-year-paper — Adda247 and Testbook run equivalent free PYQ pages if this one moves.
IB ACIO — free PYQ kit, 15+ papers. oliveboard.in/ib-acio-free-pyp-kit
NIACL AO recruitment & papers. No fixed URL — search "NIACL AO previous year paper" and "National Insurance Company AO recruitment" fresh each cycle.
IB ACIO recruitment. Notified via MHA / the IB's own recruitment cell, not a fixed URL — search "IB ACIO Grade II recruitment" fresh each cycle rather than bookmarking a page.
YouTube channels — free
PW OnlyIAS. The broadest free UPSC channel — full GS coverage, optional-subject lectures, daily current-affairs, Mains answer-writing sessions.
StudyIQ. Large free UPSC/GS channel, strong on daily current affairs and static-GS foundation videos — a solid second source alongside PW OnlyIAS.
Psyche Simplified (Sanket Jain) — @psychesimplifiedofficial. The one channel actually dedicated to UPSC Psychology optional. Full free lessons plus short strategy clips — this is your Paper 2 cold-start's fastest way in, before you're paying for anything.
Bank-exam quant/reasoning: search YouTube for Shantanu Shukla, Navneet Tiwari, or Siddharth Srivastava (all Adda247-affiliated) — consistently rated the clearest free teachers for RRB/IBPS-pattern quant and reasoning tricks.
Current affairs & consolidated notes
Daily newspaper. The Hindu or Indian Express, read daily — this is what your 20–30 min current-affairs habit should actually consist of, not an aggregator's summary of it.
Vision IAS — daily current affairs quiz. Free, no login noted at time of writing. visionias.in
Insights on India — daily current affairs. Free. insightsonindia.com
Drishti IAS — monthly current-affairs consolidation. Extensive free UPSC portal; the monthly PDF specifically needs a free account login (not a paid one) to unlock. drishtiias.com/free-downloads/monthly-current-affairs
EduRev — Psychology for UPSC Optional (notes course). Revision notes + flashcards mapped to the optional's own unit structure — free tier available, check what's still unlocked without paying before relying on it. edurev.in
Mock test platforms
RRB PO / EPFO-pattern mocks: Adda247, Testbook, Oliveboard and PracticeMock all run free RRB PO mock tests alongside their paid series — pick one and stay on it rather than fragmenting across all four.
CSE Prelims/Mains test series: most major UPSC coaching platforms (ForumIAS, Vision IAS, InsightsIAS) run one — these are paid; compare current pricing before committing, and don't start full-length CSE mocks until GS foundation is in place (Phase 4–5 per the plan, not now).
Reference books
Standard reference books (verify latest edition yourself): M. Laxmikanth — Indian Polity (EPFO Governance week + CSE GS II); Ramesh Singh — Indian Economy (EPFO Economy week + CSE GS III); NCERT XI–XII History, Geography, Economics — foundational for both EPFO and CSE static GS (see the official NCERT link above); a current UPSC Psychology optional guide, used alongside your own MSc materials for Optional Paper 1.
